      ante-swarm-bench · Independent work, not an Antigma release.

      Scripted opponents run the trail rules and policy in your browser (trail-core.js, checked tick for tick against the server's Python twin), one move per tick at the pace you pick. They are not Ante processes and no model is called. With the live switch on (and the operator's passcode) the rivals are real Ante processes on this server, calling ./trail look and ./trail plan in real time: each trail moves a cell per tick and an Ante's plan runs until its next call lands, then it drives straight.

      A swarm plays trail as one team: its members' tokens add up, and you are compared with the team's total. The round ends when you are out, when the whole team is out, or at the clock. Live, each member sees its teammates' heads and queued plans, can call ./trail team, and pins notes that only the team reads; a scripted swarm splits the tokens and keeps off its own members' paths.

      The stored Ante runs are still here: replay them on the trail replay board, or on the hill one. Other cartridges: hill · maze.

      Hardware look after spec-01 (MIT); type is Spec Mono, a subset of IBM Plex Mono (OFL). Notices.
