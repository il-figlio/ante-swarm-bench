      ante-swarm-bench · Independent work, not an Antigma release.

      The opponents run the maze eval's scripted explorer (maze-core.js, checked call for call against the eval's Python) in your browser, in the fleet shapes the eval defines. They are not Ante processes and no model is called. Every call they make is replayed from that simulation. Best times stay in this browser, and a maze you have already raced this visit does not set one.

      With the live switch on (and the operator's passcode) the rivals are real Ante processes on this server, on the model the status line names, in real time: you move as fast as you like and each Ante moves when its call lands. You see their calls, cells mapped and how far from the exit they are, never where they are, until the round ends. A live swarm plays as one team: a lead assigns each worker its own unexplored edge, and the workers post dead ends and corridors to a shared board of notes. You see only how many notes they have posted; the notes themselves come with the end of the round, with the maze. Assist adds an Ante to your team, and its notes show in the call feed as it posts them. The passcode stays in this tab and goes only to the request that starts a round.
