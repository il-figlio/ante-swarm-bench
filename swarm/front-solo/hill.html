      ante-swarm-bench · Independent work, not an Antigma release.

      Scripted opponents run the hill rules and policy in your browser (hill-core.js, checked drop for drop against the server's Python twin), one piece each per beat at the pace you pick. They are not Ante processes and no model is called. With the live switch on (and the operator's passcode) the rivals are real Ante processes on this server, calling ./hill look and ./hill drop <col> <rot> in real time; a piece any player leaves hovering past its fuse drops where it is.

      A swarm plays hill alone together: each Ante has its own well and its own piece sequence (seeded from the round's seed and its seat; yours is the round's own), and the team pools its rows. At the clock your rows are compared with the swarm's rows per well. All the garbage the swarm sends lands in your well, and yours goes round its wells in turn. The swarm is out only when every one of its wells is.

      The pieces are the cartridge's own seven: a domino, two trominoes and four pentominoes. The stored Ante runs are still here: replay them on the hill replay board, or on the trail one. Other cartridges: trail · maze.

      Hardware look after spec-01 (MIT); type is Spec Mono, a subset of IBM Plex Mono (OFL). Notices.
